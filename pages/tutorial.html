YOUR QUICK-START GUIDE

Make yourself
at home.

Find a game, share something you made, and make Games Lab feel like yours.

01

Find something to play

Open Games from the navigation. Browse the collection, search by name, or filter by genre. Visit Local games to scan common game folders on this PC and launch installed Windows games.

02

Share a community game

Open Community, sign in, and fill out the publish form. Pick a clear title, then either provide an HTTPS game page or paste a self-contained HTML game. Check your submission in My submissions.

  • Only publish games you made or have permission to share.
  • HTML is limited to 20,000 characters and plays in a sandbox.
  • Some websites block embedding; use their link to open the game separately.
03

Set up your player profile

Visit Profile to update your player name or picture. Your name and avatar are shown with your community games. Use Settings to adjust the app accent colour, compact layout, and motion.

04

Keep the app up to date

Installed versions check for releases at launch and while the app is open. If an update is available, review it and choose when to download and restart. You can also open Settings to check manually.

Community publishing setup: uploads need Firestore enabled and the project's firestore.rules published in Firebase Console. If a publish takes longer than 15 seconds, it may still complete in the background. Check My submissions; retrying the same form is safe.
